Before you order

Compare fit before choosing a size

Use this GTBuy sizing guide with the exact listing. Measure an item you already own and compare centimetres against the seller’s chart.

  • For shoes, compare foot length, removable insole length and toe-box width separately.
  • For hoodies and jackets, check flat chest width, shoulder width and sleeve length. Leave room for layers.
  • For T-shirts and jerseys, check garment length and whether the chart lists flat width or body circumference.
  • For pants and shorts, compare waistband stretch, front rise and inseam. Check the top and bottom of a matching set independently.
  • For caps and bracelets, record the head circumference range and the bracelet’s inner circumference.

Check the exact item in QC photos

When GTBuy QC photos are available, match the seller listing ID, selected size and colour before drawing conclusions from the pictures.

  • For trainers, compare heel alignment, outsole pattern and the size labels on both shoes.
  • For denim and outerwear, request close-ups of seam stitching, zipper teeth and the lining if the gallery lacks them.
  • For caps, check brim shape, embroidery and the rear adjustment strap.
  • For jewellery, look at clasp closure, surface scratches and the seller’s material description.
  • Ask about the warehouse return window and whether additional inspection views are available.

Compare the delivered cost

Use this GTBuy shipping checklist with the final parcel estimate. Ask the carrier or agent for a quote to your country and postal code.

  • Add the item price, domestic delivery and any service or currency conversion fees.
  • Compare actual parcel weight with dimensional weight and check the carrier’s billable weight rule.
  • For US, UK or EU destinations, use the applicable local currency and check whether the quote includes import charges and handling fees.
  • Check shipment tracking, insurance coverage and the claim deadline.

Frequently asked questions

What is a GTBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list organised by category. This guide links to Hipobuyfind categories and offers a checklist for sizing, QC photos and shipping.

Can a QC photo verify authenticity?

A QC photo can show visible details and condition. Authenticity requires separate evidence, such as documented provenance or professional authentication.

How do I compare international shipping quotes?

Use the same destination and packed dimensions for each quote. Compare billable weight, tracking, insurance and whether any duties or taxes are included.

Is this an official GTBuy website?

This is an independent shopping guide. Its category links open Hipobuyfind.
